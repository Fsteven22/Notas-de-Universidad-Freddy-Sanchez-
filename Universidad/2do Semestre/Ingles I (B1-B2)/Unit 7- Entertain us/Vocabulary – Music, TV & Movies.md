# 🎯 Vocabulary – Music, TV & Movies

## 🎵 Music Genres

### 🎻 Classical

> [!info] Definition and Characteristics
> 
> **Meaning:** Traditional orchestral music, usually from the 17th-19th centuries; formal and sophisticated music.
> 
> **Spanish:** Música clásica
> 
> **Pronunciation:** /ˈklæsɪkəl/
> 
> ```
> Characteristics:
> • Orchestras, symphonies, operas
> • Composers: Mozart, Beethoven, Bach
> • Instruments: violin, piano, cello, flute
> • Formal and complex compositions
> 
> Examples in conversation:
> • "I listen to classical music when I study."
> • "She plays classical piano."
> • "Do you like classical or modern music?"
> 
> Related vocabulary:
> • symphony = sinfonía
> • orchestra = orquesta
> • composer = compositor
> • concert hall = sala de conciertos
> ```

## 📺 TV Shows

### 🎭 Soap Opera

> [!info] Definition and Characteristics
> 
> **Meaning:** Serialized drama with ongoing storylines, typically melodramatic.
> 
> **Spanish:** Telenovela
> 
> **Pronunciation:** /səʊp ˈɒpərə/
> 
> ```
> Characteristics:
> • Daily/weekly episodes
> • Continuous storylines
> • Dramatic, emotional
> • Romance and family drama
> 
> Examples:
> • "My grandmother watches soap operas every afternoon."
> • "Soap operas are popular in Latin America."
> • "I'm addicted to this soap opera!"
> 
> Origin: Called "soap opera" because soap companies sponsored them
> ```

> [!info] Definition and Characteristics
> 
> **Meaning:** Humorous show designed to make people laugh; sitcom = situation comedy.
> 
> **Spanish:** Comedia
> 
> **Pronunciation:** /ˈkɒmədi/ / /ˈsɪtkɒm/
> 
> ```
> Characteristics:
> • Funny situations
> • Usually 20-30 minutes
> • Laugh track (sometimes)
> • Recurring characters
> 
> Examples:
> • "Friends is my favorite sitcom."
> • "I prefer comedies to dramas."
> • "This comedy show is hilarious!"
> 
> Related vocabulary:
> • laugh track = risa grabada
> • punchline = remate de chiste
> • stand-up comedy = comedia de pie
> ```

## 💬 Useful Expressions

### Talking About Music

> [!example] Common Phrases
> 
> ```
> Preferences:
> • "What kind of music do you like?"
> • "I'm into hip hop and R&B."
> • "I can't stand country music."
> • "Pop music isn't my thing."
> • "I'm a big fan of rock."
> 
> Describing music:
> • "This song is catchy!"
> • "The beat is amazing."
> • "I love the lyrics."
> • "It's stuck in my head."
> • "This has a good rhythm."
> 
> Activities:
> • "I'm going to a concert tonight."
> • "What are you listening to?"
> • "Turn up the music!"
> • "This song is on repeat."
> • "Have you heard their new album?"
> ```

### Talking About TV & Movies

> [!example] Common Phrases
> 
> ```
> Asking about preferences:
> • "What kind of movies do you like?"
> • "Have you seen any good shows lately?"
> • "Are you watching anything on Netflix?"
> • "What's your favorite TV series?"
> 
> Giving opinions:
> • "That movie was amazing!"
> • "I couldn't stop watching."
> • "It was boring."
> • "The ending was disappointing."
> • "I highly recommend it."
> • "It's overrated."
> 
> Watching habits:
> • "I'm binge-watching a series."
> • "I can't wait for the next episode."
> • "No spoilers, please!"
> • "I've watched it three times."
> • "It's my guilty pleasure."
> ```

> 
> ```
> Match the description to the genre:
> 
> 1. Features singing and dancing          a) Horror
> 2. Makes you laugh                       b) Documentary
> 3. Real facts and information           c) Musical
> 4. Very scary                           d) Comedy
> 5. Set in the future with technology    e) Science Fiction

> ```
