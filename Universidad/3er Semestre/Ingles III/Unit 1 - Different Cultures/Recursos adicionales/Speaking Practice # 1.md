# 📝 Guía y Guion: Flooding in Guayaquil (B1+ Level)

**Reglas de Oro para tu PPT/PPTX (Según la rúbrica):**

1. **Cero oraciones:** Solo usa palabras sueltas, viñetas cortas (máximo 3 palabras), íconos o fotos.
2. **Tiempo:** El guion dura 2:30–3:00 a ritmo tranquilo (~130 palabras/min). Tiempos por slide abajo.
3. **Contacto visual:** Las diapositivas son apoyo. No mires la pantalla: mira a tu profesor y compañeros.
4. **Transiciones:** Cada guion abre retomando el slide anterior (frases marcadas con 🔗).

## Slide 1: Introduction — 0:25

**👁️ Lo que debe ir en la diapositiva (Diseño):**

- **Foto de fondo:** Una calle muy inundada en Guayaquil.
- **Texto (Palabras clave):**
  - FLOODING IN GUAYAQUIL
  - Freddy Sánchez

**🗣️ Tu Guion (Lo que vas a decir):**

> "Good afternoon, teacher and classmates. My name is Freddy Sánchez. Today, I am going to talk about a serious problem that affects our daily lives: flooding in several areas of Guayaquil. I chose this topic because every winter our city struggles with this situation, and we urgently need real solutions."

## Slide 2: Problem Explanation — 0:40

**👁️ Lo que debe ir en la diapositiva (Diseño):**

- Dos columnas con un ícono cada una.
  - **Columna 1:** Ícono de lluvia y río. Texto: _RIVER + TIDES_
  - **Columna 2:** Ícono de basura tapando un tubo. Texto: _TRASH BLOCKS DRAINS_

**🗣️ Tu Guion (Lo que vas a decir):**

> "🔗 To solve a problem, we must first understand it. Why does Guayaquil flood? There are two main reasons. First, geography. Our city is next to the Guayas River and the Estero Salado. When heavy rain combines with high tides, the water cannot escape. Second, human behavior. People throw garbage in the streets. Plastics block the drains. When drains are blocked, streets become rivers in minutes."

## Slide 3: Proposed Solution 1 (Infrastructure) — 0:35

**👁️ Lo que debe ir en la diapositiva (Diseño):**

- Dos íconos grandes.
  - **Ícono 1:** Bomba de agua industrial. Texto: _PUMPING STATIONS_
  - **Ícono 2:** Microchip o señal de internet. Texto: _SMART SENSORS_

**🗣️ Tu Guion (Lo que vas a decir):**

> "🔗 Now that we understand the causes, let's look at solutions. I have two proposals. The first one focuses on infrastructure and technology. The city needs more powerful water pumping stations in critical areas. Also, we can install smart sensors inside the main sewers. These sensors monitor water levels and detect blockages in real time. If trash blocks a pipe, the system alerts maintenance workers so they can clean it before the rain starts."

## Slide 4: Proposed Solution 2 (Community Action) — 0:35

**👁️ Lo que debe ir en la diapositiva (Diseño):**

- Dos íconos grandes.
  - **Ícono 1:** Personas aprendiendo o un colegio. Texto: _EDUCATION CAMPAIGNS_
  - **Ícono 2:** Símbolo de prohibido botar basura. Texto: _STRICT FINES_

**🗣️ Tu Guion (Lo que vas a decir):**

> "🔗 Technology alone is not enough if we don't change our habits. My second solution is about community action. We must create education campaigns in schools and neighborhoods to teach people how to manage waste properly. In addition, the local government must apply strict fines to people who throw garbage in the streets. If we keep our streets clean, the water will flow naturally."

## Slide 5: Closing — 0:25

**👁️ Lo que debe ir en la diapositiva (Diseño):**

- **Foto de fondo:** Una foto bonita y soleada del Malecón 2000 o Puerto Santa Ana.
- **Texto central:** A CLEAN & SAFE GUAYAQUIL
- **Texto inferior:** Thank You!

**🗣️ Tu Guion (Lo que vas a decir):**

> "🔗 In conclusion, flooding in Guayaquil is a complex challenge caused by nature and human actions. However, by combining smart infrastructure with an educated community, we can control this problem. We all want a better, safer city to live in. Thank you very much for your attention. Are there any questions?"

## ❓ Preguntas probables del profesor (con respuesta corta)

- **"Who should pay for the pumping stations?"** → "The local government, with support from the national budget, because flooding affects the whole economy of the city."
- **"What can students do about this problem?"** → "We can stop throwing garbage in the streets and join cleaning campaigns in our neighborhoods."
- **"Is the problem getting worse every year?"** → "Yes, because the city grows fast and the drainage system is old. That is why we need action now."

## 🔑 Vocabulario clave (úsalo, suma nivel)

- **tide** (marea) · **drain / sewer** (alcantarilla) · **blockage** (obstrucción)
- **pumping station** (estación de bombeo) · **to monitor** (monitorear) · **in real time** (en tiempo real)
- **waste management** (manejo de basura) · **fine** (multa) · **overflow** (desbordamiento)
