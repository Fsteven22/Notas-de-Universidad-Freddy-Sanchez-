## 🧍 Object Pronouns — de sujeto a objeto

> [!note] 📋 El objeto recibe la acción
>
> El **subject pronoun** hace la acción; el **object pronoun** la recibe. Van después del verbo o de preposición.
>
> | Sujeto (PP) | Objeto (OP) | Ejemplo |
> |---|---|---|
> | I | **me** | *She asked **me**.* |
> | you | **you** | *I told **you**.* |
> | he | **him** | *We persuaded **him**.* |
> | she | **her** | *They convinced **her**.* |
> | it | **it** | *I reported **it**.* |
> | we | **us** | *He informed **us**.* |
> | they | **them** | *She asked **them**.* |
>
> **Ejemplos:**
>
> - ✅ *She asked **me** what I did.* (asked + persona → me, no I)
> - ✅ *He convinced **them** that it was a good idea.*
> - ❌ *She asked **I** what I did.* (I es sujeto, nunca va después del verbo)
>
> **Conexión con reported:** tell/say/ask/persuade/convince siempre llevan objeto — *told **him***, *persuaded **them to**…*. Si dudas entre he/him, pregunta: ¿hace o recibe? (ver [[02 - Grammar & Examples - Reported Statements & Questions]]).

## 🔄 Present Continuous — am/is/are + -ing

> [!note] 📋 Lo que está pasando ahora
>
> Fórmula: **am/is/are + verbo-ing**. El auxiliar es siempre el verbo *to be*.
>
> | Sujeto | Auxiliar | Ejemplo |
> |---|---|---|
> | I | **am** | *I **am studying**.* |
> | he/she/it | **is** | *She **is working**.* |
> | you/we/they | **are** | *They **are talking**.* |
>
> **Ortografía del -ing (3 trampas):**
>
> - Verbo en **-e** → pierde la e: *take → taking, write → writing.*
> - Consonante + vocal + consonante (1 sílaba) → dobla: *run → running, stop → stopping.*
> - **-ie** → **-y**: *lie → lying, die → dying.*
>
> **Ejemplos:**
>
> - ✅ *They **are discussing** the report right now.*
> - ✅ *She **is meeting** him tomorrow.* (plan futuro también vale)
> - ❌ *They **is discussing**…* (they pide are, no is)

## ✏️ Drills — visto en clase

> [!example] 🧪 Drill 1 — Elige el pronombre
> - *She asked (I / me) what happened.*
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> > - ***me*** — asked lleva objeto: la acción la recibe.

> [!example] 🧪 Drill 2 — Reported con objeto
> - *He persuaded (they / them) to buy one.*
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> > - ***them*** — persuade + persona + to: objeto siempre.

> [!example] 🧪 Drill 3 — Tell vs say
> - *She (said me / told me) the news.*
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> > - ***told me*** — tell exige objeto; say no (*said the news / said to me*).

> [!example] 🧪 Drill 4 — Auxiliar correcto
> - *The students (is / are) taking notes.*
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> > - ***are*** — students es plural.

> [!example] 🧪 Drill 5 — Ortografía -ing
> - *run, take, lie → ?*
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> > - ***running*** (dobla), ***taking*** (pierde la e), ***lying*** (-ie → -y).

> [!example] 🧪 Drill 6 — Todo junto
> - *Yesterday she (tell / told) (I / me) they (are / were) leaving.*
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> > - ***told me*** *they **were** leaving* — reported: pasado + objeto + backshift.
