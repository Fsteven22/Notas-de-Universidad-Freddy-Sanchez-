# 📐 Grammar & Examples - Unreal Conditionals & I Wish (U8)

## 🎯 Introducción

> [!info] 💡 ¿Por Qué Importa?
>
> Soñar con otra vida (*If I had more time… / I wish I could travel…*) usa el pasado para hablar del presente irreal — la gramática más contraintuitiva del B1+. El libro la evalúa en 8.1 (present unreal) y 8.2 (*I wish*), con accuracy check incluido.
>
> **Analogía del mundo real:** es máquina del tiempo gramatical — pasado simple para el presente soñado, pasado perfecto para el pasado perdido.
>
> | Sin Esto | Con Esto |
> |---|---|
> | *"If I have time, I travel"* (real, plano) | *"If I **had** time, I **would** travel"* (sueño) |
> | *"I want more free time"* | *"I wish I **had** more free time."* |
>
> **En esta nota:** present unreal (8.1) + *I wish* presente/futuro/pasado (8.2) + *wish vs hope*.

## 📋 Definiciones Formales

> [!note] 📋 Present unreal conditionals (8.1) — pasado que NO es pasado
>
> Fórmula en texto: *If + past simple, …would/might + base*. El pasado describe situación **imaginada**, no ayer.
>
> | Parte | Regla | Ejemplo del libro |
> |---|---|---|
> | *if*-clause | past simple (¡no es pasado real!) | *If you **saw** this job ad…* |
> | main clause | **would / might** + base | *…**would** you click? / I **might** do it.* |
> | *was/were* | en formal, ***were*** para todos (*If I **were** free…*); informal acepta *was* | *If I **were** you…* |
> | coma | *if* primero → coma; *would* primero → sin coma | *Would you apply **if you were** free?* |

> [!note] 📋 *I wish* (8.2) — el tiempo dice de cuándo es el deseo
>
> | Deseo sobre… | Estructura | Ejemplo del libro |
> |---|---|---|
> | Presente | **past simple** | *I don't have time → I wish I **had** more free time.* |
> | Futuro | **would / could** | *I can't take time off → I wish I **could take** time off.* |
> | Pasado | **past perfect** | *I didn't study → I wish I **had studied** for the exam.* |
> | Negativo→afirmativo | se voltea | *I work every weekend → I wish I **didn't work**…* |
>
> **Accuracy check:** *wish* = situación que NO puedes cambiar (*I wish you could come, but you have to work*); ***hope*** = futuro posible (*I hope **you'll** be there*).

---

## 🛠️ Método / Procedimiento (3 preguntas)

> [!note] 📋 Procedimiento General
>
> 1. **¿Real o irreal?** Si es sueño → pasado (aunque hable del presente).
> 2. **¿De cuándo el deseo?** Presente/futuro → past/would; pasado → past perfect.
> 3. **¿*wish* o *hope*?** Imposible → *wish*; posible → *hope* + futuro.
>
> **Principio clave:** el verbo te dice la época del deseo, no la del reloj.

## 🎨 Ejemplos Trabajados (Bilingües)

> [!example] 🟢 Ejemplo 1 — Present unreal (8.1C, con tu compañero)
>
> - *If you **could do** any job in the world, what job **would** you **choose**?* (Si pudieras hacer cualquier trabajo, ¿cuál elegirías?)
> - *I **wouldn't do** it if you **paid** me a million dollars.* (No lo haría ni por un millón.)

> [!example] 🟢 Ejemplo 2 — Wishes (8.2, del psicólogo + tuyos)
>
> - *I wish I **didn't have to** work such long hours.* (Ojalá no tuviera que… — presente.)
> - *I wish I **hadn't promised** to work this weekend.* (Ojalá no hubiera prometido… — pasado.)
> - *I wish I **could play** a musical instrument.* (Ojalá pudiera… — futuro/capacidad.)

## 📝 Ejercicios del Libro (grammar ref pp. 136–137)

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 1 — Present unreal choose (8.1 drill A)
>
> Elige la forma correcta:
>
> - If I had / I'd have more time, I did / I'd take piano lessons.
> - If I lived / I'd live in a smaller town, I can / I'd get to work faster.
> - If my job had paid / paid better, I felt / I'd feel happier.
> - If I could / I'd be able to live anywhere, I chose / I'd choose the Rockies.
> - Would / Had you move for a job if you didn't know / hadn't known anyone?
> - I don't think I would agree / agreed to live alone, even if the place were / had been beautiful.
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> >
> > - *If I **had**…, I**'d take**…* (*would* nunca en el *if*).
> > - *If I **lived**…, I**'d get**…*
> > - *If my job **paid** better, I**'d feel** happier.* (present unreal: *paid* + *would*).
> > - *If I **could**…, I**'d choose**…*
> > - ***Would** you move… if you **didn't know**…?*
> > - *…I **would agree**…, even if the place **were**…* (*were* formal para todos).

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 2 — Statements → wishes (8.2 drill A)
>
> Reescribe con *I wish*:
>
> - I'm leaving tomorrow. → *I wish I wasn't leaving tomorrow.* (modelo)
> - I can't speak Japanese.
> - I didn't finish my report last night.
> - I don't have any free time this weekend.
> - I can't go on vacation this summer.
> - I have an assignment to finish tonight.
> - I spent all my money on my new computer.
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> >
> > - *I wish I **could speak** Japanese.* (futuro/capacidad → *could*).
> > - *I wish I **had finished** my report last night.* (pasado → past perfect).
> > - *I wish I **had** some free time this weekend.* (presente → past simple).
> > - *I wish I **could go** on vacation…* (*could*).
> > - *I wish I **didn't have** an assignment…* (presente negativo → pasado: *didn't have*).
> > - *I wish I **hadn't spent** all my money…* (pasado → *hadn't* + participle).

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 3 — Dream job pair (8.1C, con tu compañero)
>
> Pregunta y responde las 3 (modelos):
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Respuestas modelo
> >
> > - *If you **could do** any job, what **would** you choose? → I**'d be** a drone pilot: I love cameras and travel.*
> > - *Would you **consider** a job you loved if you **were** badly paid? → Yes, if it **made** me happy — money isn't everything.*
> > - *What **would** you do with free time if you **didn't have to** work? → I**'d learn** guitar and surf.*

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 4 — Common wishes (8.2 Speaking A/B)
>
> De la lista (*different college, harder study, instrument, travel, younger/older, different job*): ¿cuáles son más comunes? + 3 tuyos con quién los tendría (modelos):
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Respuestas modelo
> >
> > - *I wish I **could travel** more — students with no money have this one.*
> > - *I wish I **had studied** harder in high school — workers with tough jobs say this.*
> > - *I wish I **were** younger — adults with **busy schedules** say this.*

## ⚠️ Errores Comunes y Principios Lógicos

> [!danger] ❌ Error 1 — *would* en el *if* (viola **if + past, would + base**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *"If I would have time…"*
> - ✅ Así sí: ***If I had** time, I **would** travel.* — *would* jamás entra al *if*.

> [!danger] ❌ Error 2 — Presente donde va pasado (viola **época del deseo**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *"I wish I **have** more time."*
> - ✅ Así sí: deseo presente → pasado (*I wish I **had***); deseo pasado → past perfect (*I wish I **had studied***).

> [!danger] ❌ Error 3 — *wish* por *hope* (viola **imposible vs posible**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *"I wish you'll come tomorrow"* (si puede venir, es *hope*).
> - ✅ Así sí: *I **hope you'll** be there* (posible); *I **wish you could** come* (imposible).
