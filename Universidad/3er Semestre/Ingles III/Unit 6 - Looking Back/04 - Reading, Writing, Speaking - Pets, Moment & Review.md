# 📰 Reading, Writing, Speaking - Pets, Moment & Review (U12)

## 🎯 Introducción

> [!info] 💡 ¿Por Qué Importa?
>
> El cierre de U12 (y del libro) combina debate con transiciones (perros), resumen+respuesta (12.4) y el national moment con el Review 4 de auto-test. Es el mini-examen final en 12.4–12.5.
>
> **Analogía del mundo real:** resumir es hacer trailer — 1 línea por postura (*Kenan vs Lucia*), tu veredicto, y preguntas que dejas picando.
>
> | Sin Esto | Con Esto |
> |---|---|
> | "Dogs are nice." | *Kenan argues X with examples; Lucia responds Y — I side with… because…* |
> | "I remember 9/11." | *Where were you when…? What do you remember most? Do we agree?* |
>
> **En esta nota:** debate pets (12.4), summary/response, national moment y Review 4.

## 📋 Definiciones Formales

> [!note] 📋 Debate: Man's Best Friend? (12.4A–D)
>
> - **Kenan (afirmativo):** perros sí — ejemplos con *For instance…* (¿menciona los de las fotos?).
> - **Lucia (negación):** servicio ≠ mascota — *service dogs aren't pets*; el punto más fuerte decide.
> - **Transiciones (12.4):** *To finish the debate… / So, in conclusion… / So, to sum up…* — se dicen **más fuerte** (énfasis de cierre).
> - **Crítica:** ¿quién ganó? ¿el punto de servicio era relevante al tema *pets*?

> [!note] 📋 Tabla 1×1 — Summary + response (12.4C–D)
>
> | Parte | Qué hace | Ejemplo (modelo del libro) |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Summary** | 1 línea por lado + veredicto | *Lucia argues dogs shouldn't be pets; Kenan shows… I side with…* |
> | **Response** | Acuerdo/desacuerdo + contraste | *The other pair didn't mention how dogs help children — important and original.* |
> | **Linkers** | Unen contraste (de B) | *Although / however / while* (subraya los del texto). |

## 🛠️ Método / Procedimiento (National moment 12.5)

> [!note] 📋 Procedimiento General — Prepare → Decide → Present
>
> 1. **Prepare:** un *national moment* de tu vida (deporte, política, farándula) + dónde estabas.
> 2. **Decide (grupo):** 1 momento + *Where were you when…? / What do you remember most? / Do we agree?*
> 3. **Present:** uno presenta sin decir qué fue — la clase adivina + ¿recuerdan distinto?
>
> **Principio clave:** describir sin nombrar — el suspenso se evalúa.

## 🎨 Ejemplos Trabajados (Bilingües)

> [!example] 🟢 Ejemplo 1 — Útiles 12.5 por fase
>
> - **Prepare:** *What about when…? / Do you remember when…? / An event I really remember was when…*
> - **Decide:** *What I remember most about… was… / Really? The way I remember it was…*
> - **Present:** *The things we remembered most about this event were… / But I remember it differently.*

> [!example] 🟢 Ejemplo 2 — Review 4 speaking (modelo overslept)
>
> - *Today I **overslept**, so I didn't have time for breakfast. I'm really hungry now. **If I hadn't overslept, I'd have had breakfast, and I wouldn't be so hungry now.*** (Me quedé dormido… Si no me hubiera quedado…, habría desayunado.)

## 📝 Ejercicios del Libro (Review 4, Units 10–12)

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 1 — Vocabulary categories (Review 4.1)
>
> Clasifica (*breakthrough, dorm, bring back, stinky, degree, bright, epic fail, phenomenon, fault, freshman, insight, knowledge, tasty, major, misunderstanding, recall, melodic, recognize, remind of, error, research, undergraduate, smooth, confusion, look back on*): Discoveries / Mistakes / College / Senses / Memory (+2 tuyas por grupo).
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> >
> > - **Discoveries:** *breakthrough, phenomenon, insight, knowledge, research.*
> > - **Mistakes:** *fault, epic fail, error, misunderstanding, confusion.*
> > - **College:** *dorm, freshman, degree, major, undergraduate.*
> > - **Senses:** *stinky, bright, tasty, melodic, smooth.*
> > - **Memory:** *bring back, recall, recognize, remind of, look back on.*

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 2 — Grammar conversation (Review 4.2)
>
> Completa: *I shouldn't have ___ (buy) this car. / Did you stop ___ (think) before you ___ (buy) it? / You could ___ (wait) a year. / If I ___ (not buy) it, I ___ (spend) all my savings. And I ___ (not have to) borrow from my parents.*
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> >
> > - *I shouldn't have **bought** this new car.* (*shouldn't have* + participle).
> > - *Did you stop **to think** about that before you **bought** it?* (*stop to* = parar para; *bought* real pasado).
> > - *You could **have waited** a year or two.* (*could have* + participle).
> > - *If I **hadn't bought** the car, I **would have spent** all my savings.* (past unreal).
> > - *And I **wouldn't have had to** borrow…* (*wouldn't have had to* — ¡triple verbo!).

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 3 — Debate + linkers (12.4B–D, modelos)
>
> Escucha, decide y escribe (modelos + contenido verificable):
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Respuestas modelo
> >
> > - *Who mentions the pictured dogs, Kenan or Lucia? → Escucha **nombres + ejemplos** (*For instance…*): marca quién dice cada foto (🎧 audio 2.53–54).*
> > - *Phrases to introduce examples: **For instance,… / For example,…**.*
> > - *Stronger case? → **Lucia**: service dogs aren't pets — that point **isn't relevant** to *pets*, so Kenan's photos don't save him. (Guíate: ¿responde al **topic**?)*
> > - *Linkers to circle in a summary: **although / however / while** (contraste).*

## ⚠️ Errores Comunes y Principios Lógicos

> [!danger] ❌ Error 1 — Resumen sin veredicto (viola **summary + response**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** resumir los dos lados y callar tu postura.
> - ✅ Así sí: 1 línea por lado + *I side with… because…* + contraste.

> [!danger] ❌ Error 2 — Nombrar el momento (viola **suspenso**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *"My national moment is the 2022 final…"* al abrir.
> - ✅ Así sí: describe (*Where were you when…?*) y que adivinen.
