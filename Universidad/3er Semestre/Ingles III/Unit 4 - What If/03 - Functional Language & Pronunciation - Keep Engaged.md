# 🗣️ Functional Language & Pronunciation - Keep Engaged (U10)

## 🎯 Introducción

> [!info] 💡 ¿Por Qué Importa?
>
> Mantener al oyente enganchado (*Don't tell me… / Let me guess! / You must be joking*) es la función de 10.3 — más las vocales largas/cortas que el libro evalúa aquí. Sin marcadores, tu historia se escucha plana; con ellos, el oyente reacciona.
>
> **Analogía del mundo real:** son los botones de reacción en vivo — *let me guess* es adivinar, *that's so sweet* es ❤️, *you can't be serious* es 😲.
>
> | Sin Esto | Con Esto |
> |---|---|
> | "They upgraded us. Nice." | *"**You won't believe this**, but they upgraded us! **Isn't that amazing?**"* |
> | Escuchas en silencio total | ***Don't tell me… / Let me guess — the beach?*** |
>
> **En esta nota:** chart keep-engaged (10.3), *anyway* (insider), vocales largas/cortas y wallet story.

## 📋 Definiciones Formales

> [!note] 📋 Tabla 1×1 — Keep your listener engaged (10.3B)
>
> | Marcador | Uso | Ejemplo del libro |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Don't tell me…!** | no me digas (suspenso) | ***Don't tell me** you booked the wrong hotel!* |
> | **Let me guess…!** | déjame adivinar | ***Let me guess** — the beach?* |
> | **That's so + adj!** | reacción (funny/strange/great/crazy/awful/sweet) | ***That's so sweet** of the owner!* |
> | **You must be joking!** | no te creo (cariñoso) | ***You must be joking** — an upgrade?!* |
> | **You can't be serious.** | no te creo (fuerte) | ***You can't be serious** — $100?!* |
> | **You won't believe…** | no vas a creer | ***You won't believe this**, but we got a reward!* |
> | **Isn't that + adj?** | ¿no es genial? | ***Isn't that amazing?** They felt sorry for us!* |
> | **Insider: *anyway*** | volver al tema | ***Anyway**, we decided to go back next summer!* |

> [!note] 📋 Vocales largas vs cortas (10.3)
>
> - **Cortas:** *add, bed, dim, sock, cub* — golpe seco.
> - **Largas:** *aid, bead, dime, soak, cube* — se estiran.
> - Drill: *hotel / kilometer / reservation / bags / amazing* → L (larga) o S (corta) por sílaba.
> - Regla: sílaba abierta (termina en vocal) suele ser **larga** (*ho-tel*).

---

## 🛠️ Método / Procedimiento (Reaccionar en 3 tiempos)

> [!note] 📋 Procedimiento General
>
> 1. **Adivina** antes del final (*Let me guess…*).
> 2. **Reacciona** con adjetivo (*That's so crazy/awful/sweet!*).
> 3. **Retoma** si te desvías (*Anyway, …*).
>
> **Principio clave:** el oyente trabaja — adivina, reacciona y devuelve la historia.

## 🎨 Ejemplos Trabajados (Bilingües)

> [!example] 🟢 Ejemplo 1 — El hotel equivocado (10.3 audio)
>
> - Huésped: *"We booked the wrong Miramar — 20 km away!"* (Reservamos el Miramar equivocado.)
> - Tú: ***Don't tell me** you slept at the station!* → ***You must be joking** — an upgrade?! **Isn't that amazing?***

> [!example] 🟢 Ejemplo 2 — Wallet $100 (10.3, modelo)
>
> - A: *I found a wallet with **$100** in it. I turned it into the police.* (Encontré billetera con $100.)
> - B: ***That's so honest** of you! **Let me guess** — reward?* → A: ***You won't believe this**, but the owner gave me **$100** reward!*

## 📝 Ejercicios del Libro (10.3 drills)

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 1 — Long or short (10.3 drill B)
>
> Marca L (larga) o S (corta) por sílaba: *hotel, kilometer, reservation, bags, amazing*.
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> >
> > - *ho-**tel*** → **S + L** (*tel* abierta = larga).
> > - *ki-**lo**-me-ter* → **S + L + S + S** (*lo* abierta).
> > - *re-ser-**va**-tion* → **S + S + L + S**.
> > - ***bags*** → **S** (cerrada).
> > - *a-**ma**-zing* → **S + L + S** (*ma* abierta).

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 2 — Complete the conversation (10.3C)
>
> Completa con el chart: *OK, ___1___? I had a job interview… / Sales: Why sales? / …I was wondering that myself, so I asked them. And, ___2___ I was in the wrong interview. / But that's not funny! You must have been embarrassed. Oh, totally! But they were embarrassed too. But ___3___. I got it. I got the job! / ___4___? I got the wrong job!*
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> >
> > - ***I mean, can I ask you something*** (1: pedir permiso para preguntar).
> > - ***—wait, do you mean*** (2: aclarar lo increíble).
> > - ***the funny thing is, they offered me*** (3: giro cómico).
> > - ***You won't believe this*** (4: remate increíble).

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 3 — Tell + react (10.3 Speaking B pair, modelos)
>
> Cuenta una historia real y reacciona a la de tu compañero (modelos):
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Respuestas modelo
> >
> > - *I found $20 in an old jacket. → **Let me guess** — you kept it? → **You won't believe this**, but I returned it and got $50 reward!*
> > - Regla del oyente: 1 adivinanza (*Let me guess*) + 1 reacción (*That's so…*) por historia.

## ⚠️ Errores Comunes y Principios Lógicos

> [!danger] ❌ Error 1 — Escuchar en silencio (viola **oyente activo**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** cara plana, cero reacción durante la historia.
> - ✅ Así sí: adivina (*Let me guess*), reacciona (*That's so…*), retoma (*Anyway*).

> [!danger] ❌ Error 2 — Vocales todas iguales (viola **larga vs corta**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *ship* por *sheep*, *full* por *fool*.
> - ✅ Así sí: cerrada = corta y seca; abierta = larga (*ho-tel*, *a-ma-zing*).
