# 📐 Grammar & Examples - Past Unreal & Past Modals (U10)

## 🎯 Introducción

> [!info] 💡 ¿Por Qué Importa?
>
> Especular sobre el pasado (*If the candy hadn't melted… / He should have checked…*) combina el tercer condicional con modales de probabilidad pasada. Es la gramática estrella de U10 (10.1 + 10.2) y la que más puntos da por precisión.
>
> **Analogía del mundo real:** es el VAR del pasado — revisas la jugada (*hadn't melted*) y dictaminas (*wouldn't have* / *should have*).
>
> | Sin Esto | Con Esto |
> |---|---|
> | *"If Newton doesn't sit there…"* (mezcla épocas) | *"If he **hadn't sat** there, he **wouldn't have discovered** gravity."* |
> | *"He should check"* (presente, tarde) | *"He **should have checked** the envelopes."* |
>
> **En esta nota:** past unreal (10.1) + *should/could/might/couldn't have* (10.2) + accuracy checks.

## 📋 Definiciones Formales

> [!note] 📋 Past unreal conditionals (10.1) — pasado que NO pasó
>
> Fórmula en texto: *If + past perfect, …would (not) + have + participle*. Habla de un pasado **imaginado**, no del real.
>
> | Parte | Regla | Ejemplo del libro |
> |---|---|---|
> | *if*-clause | **past perfect** (¡no es pasado real!) | *If the candy **hadn't melted**…* |
> | main clause | **would (not) + have** + participle | *…he **wouldn't have made** the connection.* |
> | *might* | resultado incierto | *…we **might not have** these treats today.* |
> | coma | *if* primero → coma; *would* primero → sin coma | *You **would have seen** it if you **had gone**.* |
> | pregunta/respuesta | *Would you have…? / Yes, I **would have**.* | *…**would** you **have gotten** better grades?* |

> [!note] 📋 Modals of past probability (10.2) — dictaminar el pasado
>
> | Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo del libro |
> |---|---|---|
> | **should have** + participle | criticar (debió) | *The referee **should not have allowed** the goal.* |
> | **could/might have** + participle | posibilidad perdida | *He **could have fixed** the problem. / England **might have won**.* |
> | **couldn't have** + participle | imposible | *I **couldn't have caught** that plane.* |
> | contracciones | habladas sí, escritas no | *"You **should've** told me" (speech, no writing).* |
>
> **Accuracy check:** el modal va en **afirmativo** siempre — *She **can have** gone* ❌ → *She **could have gone*** ✅.

---

## 🛠️ Método / Procedimiento (3 preguntas)

> [!note] 📋 Procedimiento General
>
> 1. **¿Especulas o criticas?** Resultado imaginado → *would have*; crítica → *should have*; posibilidad → *could/might have*.
> 2. **Arma el pasado:** *had* + participle en el *if*; *have* + participle en el main.
> 3. **Revisa:** ¿modal en afirmativo? ¿coma solo si *if* va primero?
>
> **Principio clave:** dos *have* en la frase (uno por cláusula) = tercer condicional bien armado.

## 🎨 Ejemplos Trabajados (Bilingües)

> [!example] 🟢 Ejemplo 1 — Descubrimientos (10.1C)
>
> - Newton: *If he **hadn't sat** under that apple tree, he **wouldn't have discovered** gravity.* (Si no se hubiera sentado…, no habría descubierto la gravedad.)
> - Fleming: *If Fleming **hadn't added** bacteria, he **wouldn't have invented** penicillin.* (Si no hubiera agregado…, no habría inventado.)
> - Crum: *If Crum **hadn't sliced** potatoes extra thin, he **wouldn't have created** chips.* (…no habría creado las papitas.)

> [!example] 🟢 Ejemplo 2 — Críticas y posibilidades (10.2C)
>
> - *I **shouldn't have posted** that photo.* (No debí publicar esa foto — crítica.)
> - *If I had asked permission, she **might have said** yes.* (Quizá habría dicho que sí — posibilidad.)
> - *The U.S. **might have joined** Canada without Alaska.* (historia alternativa del libro.)

## 📝 Ejercicios del Libro (grammar ref p. 138)

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 1 — Past unreal complete (10.1 drill A)
>
> Completa: *If he ___ (not have) an accident, he ___ (not arrive) late. / If she ___ (stay) in school and ___ (get) her degree, they ___ (hire) her. / If they ___ (not discover) the cause, many more people ___ (die). / If I ___ (not go) to Florida, I ___ (never meet) my wife!*
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> >
> > - *If he **hadn't had** an accident, he **wouldn't have arrived** late.*
> > - *If she **had stayed**… and **had gotten** her degree, they **would have hired** her.*
> > - *If they **hadn't discovered**…, many more people **would have died**.*
> > - *If I **hadn't gone** to Florida, I **would never have met** my wife!*

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 2 — Rewrite with past modals (10.2 drill A)
>
> Reescribe: *I chose not to study medicine → I **could have studied** medicine.* (modelo) · *It wasn't a good idea to go out before the exam. / I'm sorry I didn't call. / I didn't check the forecast. / There was a chance of that job if I tried. / It's impossible (no visa).*
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> >
> > - *I **shouldn't have gone** out the night before the exam.* (crítica).
> > - *I **should have called** last night.* (debí).
> > - *I **should have checked** the forecast.* (debí).
> > - *I **could/might have gotten** that job if I'd tried.* (posibilidad).
> > - *He **couldn't have come** (no visa).* (imposible).

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 3 — Crum + Goldman (10.1C pair, las 2 restantes)
>
> Completa con tu compañero (respuestas):
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> >
> > - *If George Crum **hadn't sliced** his potatoes extra thin, he **wouldn't have created** this popular snack.*
> > - *Sylvan Goldman **would never have thought of** this grocery item if his office chair **hadn't been** on wheels.* (main primero: sin coma.)

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 4 — Regrets + photos (10.2C + Speaking A, modelos)
>
> Responde (modelos):
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Respuestas modelo
> >
> > - *Have you ever regretted something you said? → I **shouldn't have posted** that photo — **I could have asked** first.*
> > - *Cookies on the edge: what **shouldn't** they have done? → They **shouldn't have put** the plate so close — the dog **could have jumped** on the table.*
> > - *Man with stain: what **might** have happened? → He **might not have noticed** — or it **could have been** coffee.*

## ⚠️ Errores Comunes y Principios Lógicos

> [!danger] ❌ Error 1 — *would* en el *if* pasado (viola **had en el if**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *"If he wouldn't have sat…"*
> - ✅ Así sí: ***If he hadn't sat**…, he **wouldn't have discovered**…* — *would* solo en el main.

> [!danger] ❌ Error 2 — Modal negativo con *have* (viola **modal afirmativo**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *"She can have gone"* / *"He shouldn't haven't…"*.
> - ✅ Así sí: *She **could have gone**.* — el modal no se niega con *have*.

> [!danger] ❌ Error 3 — Contracción en escritura (viola **speech ≠ writing**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *"You should've told me"* en un email formal.
> - ✅ Así sí: *should've* solo hablado; escrito → ***should have*** completo.
