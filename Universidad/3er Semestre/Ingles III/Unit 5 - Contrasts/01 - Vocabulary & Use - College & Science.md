# 📖 Vocabulary & Use - College & Science (U11)

## 🎯 Introducción

> [!info] 💡 ¿Por Qué Importa?
>
> La vida universitaria (*freshman, dorm, major*) y la ciencia (*research-based, snake oil*) son los dos mundos de vocabulario de 11.1–11.2 — y el segundo trae la tabla de familias de palabras (abstract noun → compound adjective) que el libro evalúa.
>
> **Analogía del mundo real:** las familias de palabras son matrioskas — *science → scientist → scientific → scientifically-proven*, cada una encaja en su hueco gramatical.
>
> | Sin Esto | Con Esto |
> |---|---|
> | "First year student lives in student house." | "**Freshmen** live in **dorms**; tuition needs a **degree** first." |
> | "Honey is good, science says." | "Honey is **medically approved**; carrots are **scientifically proven**." |
>
> **En esta nota:** college (11.1), science word families (11.2), *snake oil* y drills.

## 📋 Definiciones Formales

> [!note] 📋 Tabla 1×1 — College education (11.1A–B)
>
> | Palabra | Significado | Ejemplo ES → EN |
> |---|---|---|
> | **freshman / sophomore / junior / senior** | 1º/2º/3º/4º año | Estoy en primer año → *I'm a **freshman**.* |
> | **dorm** | residencia | Vive en residencia → *He moved out of the **dorm**.* |
> | **major / degree** | carrera / título | Estudio cine → *Lee is a film studies **major**.* |
> | **undergraduate / postgraduate** | pregrado / posgrado | Hace maestría → *He's getting his **master's**.* |
> | **campus / faculty / facilities** | campus / facultad / instalaciones | Profes excelentes → *Excellent **faculty**.* |
> | **extracurricular / associations** | extraacadémico / asociaciones | Soy presidente de la **society** → *president of the film **society**.* |

> [!note] 📋 Tabla 1×1 — Familias science (11.2A)
>
> | Abstract noun | Verb | Person | Adjective | Compound adjective |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | **science** | — | **scientist** | **scientific** | **scientifically-proven** |
> | **research** | **research** | **researcher** | — | **research-based** |
> | **proof** | **prove** | — | **proven** | — |
> | **base/basis** | **base** | — | **based (on facts)** | **science-based** |
> | **medicine** | — | — | **medical** | **medically-approved** |
>
> **Insider: *snake oil*** = producto milagro falso (*originally fake medicines, now anything that doesn't deliver*).

## 🛠️ Método / Procedimiento (Atacar word families)

> [!note] 📋 Procedimiento General (drill 11.2A)
>
> 1. **Mira el hueco:** ¿sustantivo, verbo, persona, adjetivo o compuesto?
> 2. **Elige la forma** de la familia (*research → researcher → research-based*).
> 3. **Verifica colocación:** *scientifically-proven* (adverbio + participio), *science-based* (sustant + based).
>
> **Principio clave:** el hueco manda la forma — nunca la palabra suelta.

## 🎨 Ejemplos Trabajados (Bilingües)

> [!example] 🟢 Ejemplo 1 — Lee y Allie (11.1)
>
> - *Lee will never forget **getting** his acceptance letter; he's a film studies **major**.* (Lee jamás olvidará recibir su carta; estudia cine.)
> - *Allie was a **freshman**; when she stopped **partying**, her **grades** went up.* (Allie era de primero; al dejar la fiesta, subieron sus notas.)

> [!example] 🟢 Ejemplo 2 — Folk remedies (11.2)
>
> - *Carrots are **scientifically proven**; honey is **medically approved**.* (Zanahoria probada; miel aprobada.)
> - *Cheese before bed is **snake oil** — no **basis** for nightmares.* (Queso y pesadillas: cuento sin base.)

## 📝 Ejercicios del Libro (11.1–11.2 drills)

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 1 — College definitions (11.1 drill A)
>
> Une (*dorm, faculty, freshman, grade, major, semester, society*): *teachers in a department / first-year student / organized by students / building where students live / main focus of studies / letter showing how good your work is.*
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> >
> > - ***faculty*** — profesores del departamento.
> > - ***freshman*** — estudiante de primer año.
> > - ***society*** — organizada por estudiantes.
> > - ***dorm*** — edificio donde viven.
> > - ***major*** — foco principal de estudios.
> > - ***grade*** — letra/número de tu rendimiento.

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 2 — Word forms (11.2 drill B)
>
> Completa con la forma correcta: *The researchers were not able to ___ (proof) that cheese gives nightmares. / It is a ___ (science) fact. / ___ (medicine) studies suggest it. / Honey has been ___ (science) proven. / Studies ___ (base) on few patients are unreliable.*
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> >
> > - ***prove*** (verbo tras *able to*).
> > - ***scientific*** (adjetivo antes de *fact*).
> > - ***Medical*** (adjetivo antes de *studies*).
> > - ***scientifically*** (adverbio antes de *proven*).
> > - ***based*** (*studies based on…*).

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 3 — Brochure + freshman life (11.1A/C, modelos)
>
> Responde con el brochure y tu vida (modelos):
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Respuestas modelo
> >
> > - *Academic vs social: **labs and libraries** = academic; **clubs and dorms** = social. Lee loves **societies** (social); Allie reencontró los **grades** (academic).*
> > - *Best/worst freshman thing: best = **societies and friends**; worst = **partying hurt my grades**. Dorm or apartment as sophomore? **Dorm: cheaper + near classes**.*
> > - *Coolest major: **oceanography** — field trips at sea. Would I study it? **I could try** marine biology first.*

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 4 — What does science say? (11.2B veredictos)
>
> Da el veredicto de Dr. Wendt (del audio script):
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Solución
> >
> > - *Carrots/night vision: **backed by real science** — vitamin A **is scientifically proven**.*
> > - *Honey/cough: **true** — tested, **proof** it works like medicine.*
> > - *Cheese/nightmares: **no basis** — heavy meals **disturb sleep / make you remember dreams**, but cheese doesn't cause them.*

## ⚠️ Errores Comunes y Principios Lógicos

> [!danger] ❌ Error 1 — Forma vecina (viola **hueco manda**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *"a science fact"* / *"researcher-based"*.
> - ✅ Así sí: ***scientific** fact*, ***research-based*** — adjetivo simple o compuesto, según el hueco.

> [!danger] ❌ Error 2 — *freshman* plural inventado (viola **irregular**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *"freshmans"*.
> - ✅ Así sí: ***freshmen*** (como *men*).
