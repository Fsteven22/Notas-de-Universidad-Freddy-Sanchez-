> 
> **Spanish:** Aplicar, postular, solicitar
> 
> **Pronunciation:** /əˈplaɪ/
> 
> ```
> Grammar:
> • apply for + job/position
> • apply to + company/university
> 
> Examples:
> • "I'm applying for a new job."
>   (Estoy aplicando/postulando a un nuevo trabajo)
> 
> • "She applied to three universities."
>   (Ella aplicó a tres universidades)
> 
> • "Have you applied for the position yet?"
>   (¿Ya has aplicado a la posición?)

> • applicant (noun) = solicitante, postulante
> 
> Common phrases:
> • "job application" = solicitud de empleo
> • "application form" = formulario de solicitud
> • "apply for a scholarship" = solicitar una beca
> ```

### 🔥 Fire (verb)

> 
> **Meaning:** To dismiss someone from their job; to terminate employment.
> 
> **Spanish:** Despedir, echar
> 
> **Pronunciation:** /faɪər/
> 
> ```
> Examples:
> • "He was fired for being late repeatedly."
>   (Fue despedido por llegar tarde repetidamente)
> 
> • "The company fired 100 employees."
>   (La compañía despidió a 100 empleados)
> 
> • "She got fired last week."
>   (Ella fue despedida la semana pasada)
> 
> • "They can't fire you without reason."
>   (No pueden despedirte sin razón)
>

> • dismiss = despedir (formal)
> • terminate = terminar el contrato
> • let go = dejar ir (más suave)
> • lay off = despedir por recortes (no por desempeño)
> 
> Common phrases:
> • "get fired" = ser despedido
> • "fire someone" = despedir a alguien
> • "firing/dismissal" = despido
> 
> Note: "Fire" is direct/informal. In professional contexts,
> use "dismiss" or "terminate employment"
