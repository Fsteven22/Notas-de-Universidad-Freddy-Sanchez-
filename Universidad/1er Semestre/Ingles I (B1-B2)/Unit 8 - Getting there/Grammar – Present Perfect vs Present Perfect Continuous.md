# 📘 Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

## 🎯 Introducción

> [!info] 💡 ¿De qué trata esta nota?
>
> Los dos tiempos que conectan el pasado con el presente: el **Simple** (resultado/experiencia) y el **Continuous** (duración/proceso). La diferencia decide lecciones enteras de Inglés III.
>
> | Tiempo | Pregunta que responde | Ejemplo |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Present Perfect Simple** | ¿Qué pasó (y qué importa ahora)? | *I have finished.* |
> | **Present Perfect Continuous** | ¿Cuánto lleva pasando? | *I have been waiting.* |
>
> ![[Diagramas/Grammar_–_Present_Perfect_vs_Present_Perfect_Continuous_01.svg]]

## 📘 Present Perfect Simple — forma y usos

> [!success] 📋 Forma: have/has + past participle
>
> - **Afirmativo:** I/you/we/they + *have* + participio; he/she/it + *has* + participio. (*I have finished. She has visited Paris three times.*)
> - **Negativo:** *have not (haven't) / has not (hasn't)* + participio. (*I haven't seen that movie.*)
> - **Pregunta:** *Have/Has* + sujeto + participio. (*Have you finished? Has she arrived yet?*)
> - **Respuesta corta:** *Yes, I have. / No, he hasn't.*
>
> ### 🎯 Los 4 usos (con su test)
>
> 1. **Experiencia de vida** (tiempo NO especificado especificado): *I have read that book. She has visited Japan.* Test: ¿puedes agregar *yesterday*? Si sí → NO es este uso.
> 2. **Periodo no terminado** (today, this week, so far): *They have bought a new car (this year).*
> 3. **Resultado visible ahora**: *He has eaten all the cookies!* (migajas como evidencia).
> 4. **Conteo/logros**: *She has written three books so far. They have visited Paris twice.*
>
> **Regla de oro:** marcador de pasado específico (*yesterday, last year, ago, in 2020*) → **Past Simple**, nunca Present Perfect. (*I went to Paris yesterday.* ✔ / *I have been to Paris yesterday.* ❌)

## 🔄 Present Perfect Continuous — forma y usos

> [!success] 📋 Forma: have/has + been + verbo-ing
>
> - *I have been studying English for three years. She has been working since January.*
> - **Pregunta duración:** *How long have you been waiting?*
>
> ### 🎯 Cuándo usarlo (y cuándo NO)
>
> - **Duración en curso:** la acción sigue o acaba de parar y se ve el efecto. (*The ground is wet. It has been raining.*)
> - **Procesos repetidos:** *I have been reading this book for weeks (but I haven't finished it).*
> - **NUNCA con stative verbs:** *know, have (poseer), believe, want, like* → Simple. (*I have known her.* ✔ / *I have been knowing her.* ❌)
> - **NUNCA para contar terminados:** *He has written five emails.* ✔ (no *has been writing* para contar)
>
> **FOR vs SINCE:** *for* = duración (for 5 years, for a long time — responde *How long?*); *since* = punto de partida (since 2020, since Monday — responde *Since when?*).

## ⚖️ Decisión: Simple o Continuous

> [!note] 📋 Comparación en 1 tabla
>
> | Aspecto | Simple | Continuous |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Foco** | Resultado, conteo | Duración, proceso |
> | **Pista** | *twice, already, yet, just* | *for, since, how long, all morning* |
> | **Stative verbs** | ✅ Siempre | ❌ Nunca |
> | **Ejemplo** | *She has visited Paris twice.* | *She has been living here since 2020.* |

## ⚠️ Errores de hispanohablantes

> [!danger] ❌ Error 1: Present Perfect con pasado específico (viola **conexión con el presente**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *I have seen him yesterday.* / *She has visited London last year.*
> - ✅ Así sí: *I saw him yesterday.* / *She visited London last year.*
> - **Por qué:** el español ("he visto ayer") lo permite; el inglés no. Marcador específico → Past Simple. Siempre.

> [!danger] ❌ Error 2: Continuous con stative verbs (viola **estados no progresan**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *I have been knowing her. She has been having a car.*
> - ✅ Así sí: *I have known her. She has had a car.*
> - **Stative comunes:** know, believe, want, like, need, have (poseer), own. Excepción: *think* como proceso (*I have been thinking about it* ✔).

> [!danger] ❌ Error 3: FOR/SINCE cruzados (viola **duración vs punto**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *since 5 years / for 2020.*
> - ✅ Así sí: *for 5 years* (¿cuánto?) / *since 2020* (¿desde cuándo?).

> [!danger] ❌ Error 4: Participio inventado (viola **irregulares se memorizan**)
>
> - ❌ **EL ERROR ESTÁ AQUÍ:** *I have breaked / writed / goed.*
> - ✅ Así sí: *broken / written / gone.* Los 20 irregulares top van de memoria, sin atajo.

## 📝 Ejercicios de práctica

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 1 — Simple o Continuous
>
> Completa con Present Perfect o Continuous:
>
> 1. I __________ (study) English for three years.
> 2. She __________ (write) three books so far.
> 3. How long __________ you __________ (wait)?
> 4. They __________ (visit) Paris twice.
> 5. We __________ (work) on this project since January.
> 6. He __________ (eat) all the cookies!
> 7. I __________ (read) this book for weeks, but I haven't finished it.
> 8. You look tired. __________ you __________ (exercise)?
> 9. She __________ (know) him since childhood.
> 10. The ground is wet. It __________ (rain).
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Respuestas — Ejercicio 1
> >
> > 1. *have been studying* (duración) · 2. *has written* (logro contado) · 3. *have you been waiting* (duración) · 4. *have visited* (experiencias) · 5. *have been working* (en curso) · 6. *has eaten* (resultado visible) · 7. *have been reading* (proceso) · 8. *Have you been exercising* (evidencia) · 9. *has known* (stative) · 10. *has been raining* (efecto ahora).

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 2 — Corrige el error
>
> 11. I have been knowing her for ten years.
> 12. She has been visiting Paris three times.
> 13. How long do you live here?
> 14. They have been finishing their homework.
> 15. We are working here since 2020.
> 16. He has been writing five emails today.
> 17. I have been to the gym yesterday.
> 18. She has been having a car for five years.
> 19. Have you been ever to Japan?
> 20. They have been already left.
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Respuestas — Ejercicio 2
> >
> > 11. *I have known her* (stative) · 12. *She has visited* (conteo) · 13. *How long have you lived/been living* · 14. *They have finished* (completado) · 15. *We have been working/have worked* · 16. *He has written* (conteo) · 17. *I went* (yesterday → simple) · 18. *She has had* (stative) · 19. *Have you ever been* (orden) · 20. *They have already left* (completado).

> [!example] ✏️ Ejercicio 3 — Conversaciones
>
> - A: You look exhausted! What __________ you __________ (do)? B: I __________ (run) for an hour. A: How far __________ you __________ (run)? B: I __________ (run) about 8 kilometers.
> - A: Where's Tom? B: He's not here. He __________ (go) to the store. A: How long __________ he __________ (be) gone? B: He __________ (be) away for 30 minutes.
> - A: Your room is very clean! B: Thanks! I __________ (clean) it all morning. A: __________ you __________ (finish) yet? B: Yes, I __________ (do) everything!
> - A: I'm looking for Maria. __________ you __________ (see) her? B: Yes, she __________ (be) in the library since 2 PM. A: What __________ she __________ (do)? B: She __________ (study) for her exam.
>
> > [!success]- ✅ Respuestas — Ejercicio 3
> >
> > Conv.1: *have you been doing / have been running / have you run / have run* · Conv.2: *has gone / has he been / has been* · Conv.3: *have been cleaning / have you finished / have done* · Conv.4: *have you seen / has been / has she been doing / has been studying*.

## 📚 Glosario mínimo

> [!quote] 📖 Solo lo que confunde
>
> | Término | Explicación |
> |---|---|
> | **Stative verb** | Verbo de estado (know, want, have): nunca en continuous. |
> | **Past participle** | Tercera forma (*written, gone, been*): va con have/has. |
> | **For / Since** | *For* mide duración; *since* marca el inicio. |
> | **Yet / Already / Just** | *Yet* (¿ya?/todavía no), *already* (ya), *just* (acabar de) — compañeros del Simple. |
> | **Ever / Never** | Experiencias de vida: *Have you ever been...?* |
