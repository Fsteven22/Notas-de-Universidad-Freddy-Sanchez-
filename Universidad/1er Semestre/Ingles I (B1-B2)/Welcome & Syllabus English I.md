# English I — Welcome & Syllabus

## 🎉 Welcome to English I!

> [!info] 👋 About this course
>
> Welcome! English I develops your ability to produce simple academic paragraphs, identify arguments in oral and written communication, and express your own views on social, academic and professional topics. It also covers vocabulary for comparing past and present, describing books and films, and recounting past events.
>
> | Detail | Info |
> |---|---|
> | **Course** | English I |
> | **Code** | IDIG1006 |
> | **Institution** | ESPOL |
> | **Credits** | 3.8 ECTS |
> | **Hours** | 2h lecture · 2h practice · 2h autonomous |
> | **Type** | Mandatory |
> | **Prerequisite** | None |
> | **Coordinator** | María Cristina Faytong Real |
> | **Textbook** | Evolve Level 3B Student's Book |

## 🎯 Course Objectives

> [!note] 📌 What will we achieve?
>
> | # | Learning Outcome |
> |---|---|
> | 1 | Understand main ideas in monologues and dialogues on familiar topics |
> | 2 | Examine information from texts on familiar, social and educational topics |
> | 3 | Develop oral communication through dialogues, interviews and simple presentations |
> | 4 | Write short texts on everyday topics using learned vocabulary and grammar |
>
> **General skill:** Habilidad para comunicarse efectivamente en inglés con diversas audiencias.
